Most voice agents stop getting better the day they go live.

Building the agent is the easy part. What breaks teams is everything after launch — failures hidden in long-tail calls, manual QA, and changes that ship without proof.

Failures hide in the long tail

The same issues can show up in a few calls each week and still go unnoticed for months.

QA is a sampling problem

Most teams review a tiny fraction of calls and assume it reflects reality. It rarely does.

Fixes are guesses

Someone edits a prompt, ships it, and waits. There is no reliable way to prove it helped.

The stack is disconnected

Building, monitoring, evaluating and improving happen in different tools. The loop never closes.
